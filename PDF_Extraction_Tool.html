ExtractaPDF Pro — Comprehensive User Guide

Complete manual for Table Extraction, PDF Page Utilities, and Comments & Markups Management

100% In-Browser Privacy & Zero Installation Architecture

ExtractaPDF Pro executes entirely inside your browser tab without any back-end servers, Node.js, Python, or command-line requirements. PDF rendering, OCR coordinates, annotation parsing, page operations, and Excel file synthesis all execute locally in your computer's memory. Your sensitive engineering drawings and contract documents are never uploaded anywhere.

1

Three Integrated Workspaces

Switch seamlessly between workspaces using the segmented control in the top navigation bar:

Table Extraction & Excel
Extract structured tabular data from PDF sheets using Instant Local parsing or Multimodal AI Vision into interactive spreadsheets.
PDF Page Tools
Extract custom page ranges, rotate pages clockwise/counter-clockwise, merge multiple PDFs, or split documents into individual sheets.
Review Comments
Dual-pass extraction of customer markups, callouts, and stamps with an interactive pinpoint drawing viewer and review management workflow.
2

Table Extraction Engine & Extraction Presets

⚡ Instant Local Engine (Free & Offline):

Extracts digital vector text layers in milliseconds with 0 API keys. Best for born-digital PDFs generated from AutoCAD, Revit, Excel, or Word.

✦ Multimodal AI Vision Engine:

Supports Google Gemini (Gemini 3.8 Flash via the fast Interactions API) and OpenAI (GPT-4o and GPT-4o-mini). Best for scanned paper drawings, photos, handwritten notes, complex merged headers, and dense schedules.

Tailored Extraction Presets:

  • Structural BOQ & Schedules: Preserves engineering symbols (Ø, ±), signs, units (mm, kN, kg), decimals, and prevents OCR confusion (e.g. 0 vs O, 1 vs I).
  • Wide Table / Many Dense Columns: Strict column exhaustiveness rules prevent narrow columns (Item #, Pos, Mark, Qty, Remarks) from being skipped or merged.
  • Financial Statements & Balance Sheets: Maintains line item hierarchy, monetary units, parenthesized negatives, and accounting headers.
  • Invoices & Purchase Orders: Captures itemized descriptions, unit rates, tax amounts, and totals cleanly.
  • Scientific Data Matrices: Captures bracketed units, sample IDs, standard deviations, and exponents.

Flexible Extraction Scope (Selection vs Full Page vs Page Numbers):

Choose between Selection (draw marquee crop box with 8 resize handles), Current Page (extract entire active page), or Page Nos. (input specific pages like 1, 3, 5-8 or click All Pages to extract tables across multiple document pages at once with automatic multi-table tabs).

3

Spreadsheet Studio, Find & Replace, and Excel Export

  • Interactive Grid: Double click any cell to edit. Add/remove rows and columns, clean blank lines, or transpose rows and columns.
  • Find & Replace: Rapidly find and batch-replace recurring text or OCR misreads across the entire active sheet.
  • Multi-Table Worksheets: Each extracted table creates a separate tab. Export creates a multi-sheet workbook with automatic deduplicated tab names respecting Excel's 31-character limits.
  • Raw Text @ Formatting: Excel cells are preserved as pure text, preventing Excel from stripping leading zeros (0042) or converting fractions and symbols into dates.
  • Multiple Export Formats: Export to styled Excel (.xlsx), CSV, Copy for Excel (TSV for instant clipboard paste), or Copy Markdown (for GitHub/Notion).
  • API Key Privacy: View or mask your API keys with the eye toggle, or click "Clear Key" to wipe it from memory immediately.
4

PDF Page Tools (Extract, Rotate, Merge, Split)

  • Robust Hybrid Extraction Engine: Uses direct lossless PDF vector page extraction with an automatic 2.5x print-grade canvas rendering fallback. This guarantees successful page exports even for encrypted, protected, or non-standard PDFs without decryption errors.
  • Extract Pages: Specify individual pages or custom ranges (e.g. 1, 3, 5-8) to create a clean, standalone PDF.
  • Rotate Pages: Rotate all pages or target specific pages by 90°, 180°, or 270° clockwise or counter-clockwise.
  • Merge Multiple PDFs: Select and queue multiple PDF documents, view the merged list, and synthesize them into a combined PDF locally.
  • Split PDF: Extract individual standalone single-page PDFs instantly.
5

Customer & Consultant Comments Extractor

Designed specifically for engineering, architectural, and construction project drawing reviews:

🔍 Dual-Pass Annotation Scanner

Scans standard PDF.js annotations (Text, FreeText, Highlights, Callouts, Revision Stamps) and deep stream dictionaries to guarantee 100% extraction of comments, author metadata, and coordinates.

🎯 Live Pinpointing Drawing Viewer

High-DPI drawing canvas with clickable bounding pins for every comment. Clicking "View on Drawing" auto-scrolls the sheet directly to the markup with a luminous focus ring.

  • Contractor Response & Status Workflow: Enter contractor responses/actions taken directly in each comment card and track resolution status (Pending, Addressed, Verified, Rejected).
  • Live Filters & Search: Instant search across comment text, author, and ID. Filter by sheet page (showing live markup counts per page) or review status.
  • Corporate Excel Log (CUSTOMER / CONSULTANT COMMENTS): Exports a presentation-ready 8-column review log with executive dark navy banners, metadata blocks, and auto-filters.
6

Universal Session Reset & Privacy Safeguards

Clicking the Reset button in the top navigation bar completely purges all browser memory across all three workspaces:

  • Cancels all active rendering, OCR, and AI tasks.
  • Purges loaded PDF documents, page thumbnails, crops, spreadsheets, and table tabs.
  • Destroys and blanks the drawing canvas, annotation overlays, and comment review cards in Customer & Consultant Comments Extractor.
  • Clears file picker inputs and restores all tool settings to their original pristine state.
